KOODAL is an ancient Tamil word (கூடல்) meaning "a place where people come together."

More than two thousand years ago, our ancestors used this word to describe gatherings of people, the confluence of rivers, and the meeting of hearts. We build tools that help families stay connected, no matter the distance or the kind of day they're having.

A note on support, not medical care

Parent Companion, Small Moments of Joy, and everything else we make are just reflection tools — quiet spaces to notice and write down small moments. They're not medical devices, they can't diagnose anything, and they're not a substitute for therapy or clinical care.

If you're worried about your child's development, your own wellbeing, or any health matter, please talk to a doctor, therapist, or other qualified professional. We're here to support that journey, not replace it.

FAQ

Common questions

A few things parents ask us before getting started.

Is my data really private?

Yes. Parent Companion has no account, no login, and no cloud sync — everything you write stays on your own device. We don't collect, see, or store your reflections anywhere.

Is this a medical or diagnostic tool?

No. Our tools are reflection spaces, not medical devices — they can't diagnose anything and aren't a substitute for therapy or clinical care. See our full disclaimer for details.

What devices does Parent Companion work on?

We're finalizing the supported device list. Write to us at hellokoodalstudio@gmail.com and we'll confirm compatibility with your device.

Can I get a refund?

Our refund policy is being finalized. If something isn't right, email us — we'll work it out with you personally.

Is Small Moments of Joy really free?

Yes, completely — no card, no account, no catch. It's our way of giving something useful to every family, whether or not you ever try Parent Companion.
