KOODAL

Where People Come Together.

KOODAL is an ancient Tamil word (கூடல்) meaning "a place where people come together."

More than two thousand years ago, our ancestors used this word to describe gatherings of people, the confluence of rivers, and the meeting of hearts.

We build human-centred experiences — quiet companions that help families stay connected across distance, time, and circumstance.
